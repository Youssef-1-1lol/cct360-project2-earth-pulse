BEHIND THE PULSE

Real data.
A different perspective.

Earth Pulse turns a catalogue of recent earthquakes into something you can explore. Every point starts with a real event in the USGS feed.

01 / SCALE

Magnitude becomes size.

Larger magnitudes draw larger circles. Ring size is an illustrative scale, not a measure of shaking or damage.

02 / COLOUR

Depth sets the tone.

Orange marks events shallower than 70 km, green marks 70–300 km, and blue marks events deeper than 300 km.

03 / TIME

Freshness leaves a trace.

Recent events appear more opaque. The selected event has its own pulse speed, shaped by its magnitude.

04 / CONTEXT

Look beyond the dots.

This shows earthquakes of magnitude 2.5+ reported by USGS, not every earthquake worldwide. Data can be revised; small events are detected unevenly.
